## Learning Objectives

In this book you will learn the principles and practice of **Systems Administration
and Maintenance** across on-premises Linux and the AWS cloud. The book is split
into two strands:

- **Parts I–IV** are the **lectures** — concise theory, diagrams, and checkpoints.
- **Part V** are the **laboratory exercises** — one AWS training module per week,
  each with a short hands-on activity, a named artifact, and 5 checkpoint questions.
